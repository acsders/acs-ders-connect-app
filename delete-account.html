ACS DERS CONNECT

Request Account Deletion

Students and staff can request deletion of their ACS DERS CONNECT account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Visit the official ACS DERS website to find the institution's contact details. Ask the campus administrator to delete your ACS DERS CONNECT account. Include your app username, but never send your password.

Visit the official ACS DERS website

What happens to your data?

After verifying your identity, the administrator will review the request and delete eligible account information and personal data.

Some attendance or academic records may need to be retained for legitimate legal or institutional reasons. The institution should explain what is retained, why, and for how long.

Processing your request

This page explains how to request deletion. It does not automatically delete an account. The institution must provide a working way to receive and process deletion requests.
