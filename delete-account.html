Request Account Deletion

Students, parents/guardians and staff of ACS DERS can ask us to delete an ACS DERS CONNECT account and the personal data linked to it. You do not need to log in to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the request form below. Ask only for what is needed – never send your password.
  2. You will see a reference number only if your request was delivered to the school administrator.
  3. The administrator verifies the request (see below). Nothing is deleted before verification.
  4. After verification, eligible personal data is deleted by the school administrator, subject to applicable requirements and any justified retention. We will confirm with you when it is done.

Submitting the form does not delete anything automatically.

How we verify your identity

  • Students under 18: the request should be made by a parent or guardian. Choose "Parent / guardian of a student" and enter the student's name.
  • Parents / guardians: we will check the request against the parent/guardian details already held on the school's records, and may contact you or the school office to confirm.
  • Students 18 or older and staff: we will confirm using the contact details already held on school records.
  • We may ask for additional confirmation. We will never ask for your password.
Will be deleted

Personal data removed

  • The app login account and credentials
  • Profile details held for the app (name, photo, contact details)
  • App preferences and notification/device tokens
  • Content created in the app that is not part of an institutional record

Deletion happens after identity verification, subject to applicable requirements and any justified retention.

May be retained

Records kept where justified

  • Academic, attendance and examination records
  • Fee, payment and accounting records
  • Disciplinary and safeguarding records
  • Staff employment and payroll records
  • Security and audit logs

Data retention

Some records may need to be kept for legal, regulatory or institutional reasons. How long each type of record is kept is set by the institution's records-retention policy. Records that are kept are used only for those purposes and are not used for anything else.

If you want to know whether a particular record will be kept and for how long, include this in the notes field of the form and the administrator will tell you when they respond.

Deletion request form
